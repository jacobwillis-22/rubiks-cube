Step 1 of 7

Make the white cross

Find the four white edge pieces and bring them around the white center.

Quick tip

Match each edge's side color with the center of that same color. The cross should look like a white plus, with all four side colors lined up too.

Beginner method
  1. 1. White cross
    Picture the goalA white plus sign around the center.

    Goal: Make a white plus sign around the white center, and match each edge’s other color with its side center.

    How: Find a white edge piece (it has two colors). Bring it to the white face, line up its side color with the matching center, then place it beside the white center. Repeat for all four edges. Try to keep already-correct edges in place.

    There isn’t one fixed algorithm for this step; solve the four edges one at a time.

    🔎
    1. Find an edge
    Look for a piece with white and one other color.
    ↔️
    2. Match the side
    Line its other color up with the same-colored center.
    ↻
    3. Bring white in
    Turn that layer to put the white sticker beside the white center.
  2. 2. White corners
    Picture the goalAll nine stickers on the white face are white.

    Goal: Complete the entire white face and the first layer’s side colors.

    How: Find a corner with white on it in the top layer. Match its two side colors to the centers where it belongs. Hold that target slot at the front-right, with the corner above it, then repeat the sequence until the corner is inserted correctly. Turn the top layer to set up the next corner.

    If a white corner is stuck in the bottom layer incorrectly, use the sequence to bring it out, then set it up above its correct slot.

  5. 5. Match yellow edges
    Picture the goalThe yellow cross stays on top; each edge's side color matches its center.

    Goal: Make the four yellow-cross edge side colors match the side centers.

    How: Turn U and check the side stickers on the yellow edges. If two neighboring edges match, hold those matched edges at the back and right, then use the sequence.   6. 6. Position yellow corners
    Picture the goalEach corner belongs between the correct three center colors—even if yellow faces sideways.

    Goal: Put each yellow corner in the correct location. Its yellow sticker may point sideways for now; that is okay.

    How: A corner is in the correct location when its three colors belong between those three centers. Find one correctly located corner and hold it at the front-right of the top layer. Do the algorithm, check the corners, and repeat as needed. If none is correct, do it once from any angle first.

  7. 7. Twist yellow corners
    Picture the goalTurn every yellow corner sticker upward, then line up the sides.

    Goal: Turn all yellow corner stickers upward without changing the cube’s final arrangement.

    How: Keep yellow on top. Put an unsolved yellow corner at the top-front-right position and repeat the sequence until its yellow sticker faces up (usually 2 or 4 repetitions). Then turn only the top layer (U) to bring the next unsolved corner to that same position and repeat. The cube may look scrambled during this step—keep going and do not rotate the whole cube.
